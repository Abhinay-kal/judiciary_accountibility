## Overview

This tool helps Indian citizens generate lawful **Right to Information (RTI)** requests to Public Information Officers (PIOs) across Indian courts. RTI is a fundamental right guaranteed under the **Right to Information Act, 2005**.

**Key Features:**
- Pre-built templates for common court-related requests
- Automatic jurisdiction routing to correct PIO
- Legal compliance validation
- Export to multiple formats (PDF, Word, postal)
- Optional submission tracking
- Multilingual support

---

## What is an RTI Request?

The **Right to Information Act, 2005** empowers Indian citizens to seek information held by public authorities, including courts, within 30 days.

### Who Can File an RTI?
- Any Indian citizen
- No eligibility restrictions
- No fees (except copying charges: ₹2/page)

### What Information Can You Request?
✓ Court case status and orders  
✓ Missing records and documents  
✓ Case transfer history  
✓ Administrative procedures  
✓ Court statistics  
✓ PIO contact information  

### What Cannot Be Requested Under RTI?
✗ Personal information of judges (privacy protected)  
✗ Information affecting national security  
✗ Information about pending criminal cases  
✗ Third-party personal data (without consent)  
✗ Cabinet secrets and deliberations  

### Legal Timeline
- **Filing:** Submit RTI request to correct PIO
- **Acknowledgment:** PIO acknowledges within 5 days
- **Decision:** PIO provides response within 30 days (can extend 20 more days)
- **First Appeal:** Within 90 days if denied
- **Second Appeal:** To Central Information Commissioner

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Select RTI Type

Choose the type of information you need:

#### Common RTI Types:

1. **Missing Records**
   - Use when: Court documents are unavailable or incomplete
   - Example: Missing judgment copy
   - Timeline: 30 days from submission

2. **Transfer History**
   - Use when: You need record of case transfers between courts
   - Example: Case transferred from district to high court
   - Includes: All transfer orders and dates

3. **Transcripts**
   - Use when: You need hearing proceedings or testimony records
   - Example: Court hearing transcript from 2022
   - Availability: Court may charge additional fees

4. **Pending Orders**
   - Use when: You're seeking current status of pending orders
   - Example: Status of warrant case
   - Timeline: 30 days

5. **Case Listing**
   - Use when: You need information about case history
   - Example: All cases involving specific parties
   - Scope: Can be extensive - be specific

6. **Administrative Delays**
   - Use when: Investigating reasons for case postponements
   - Example: Why hearings were adjourned 5 times
   - Transparency: Court procedures and reasons

7. **Discrepancies**
   - Use when: Information in records doesn't match
   - Example: Case number appears differently in two orders
   - Clarity: Helps identify clerical errors

### Step 2: Enter Case Details

Provide information about the case you're inquiring about:

```
Case Number:        123/2023
Case Year:          2023
Court Name:         District Court, Mumbai
Court Level:        District Court
State:              Maharashtra
Judge Name:         Justice Ramesh Kumar (optional)
Case Status:        Pending / Disposed / Appealed
```

**Tips for Case Details:**
- Use exact case number format from court documents
- If unsure about format, consult court website
- Lower court case number = faster processing
- Include all relevant court names

### Step 3: Enter Applicant Information

Provide your contact details (used for PIO's acknowledgment):

```
Full Name:          John Doe
Email Address:      john.doe@example.com
Phone Number:       +91-9876-543-210
Residential Address: 123 Main Street, Mumbai
City:               Mumbai
State:              Maharashtra
Postal Code:        400001
Language Preference: English / हिंदी / Other
```

**Privacy Notes:**
- Your email is used only by the PIO
- Your address helps PIO send physical copy if needed
- You can use a pseudonym (but PIO may refuse)
- Consider using a clean email address

### Step 4: Define Information Needs

Be **specific** about what information you need:

❌ **Too Vague:**
- "I want information about my case"

✅ **Specific:**
- "I request: (1) All hearing dates since 2023, (2) List of adjournments with reasons, (3) Current status"

**Guidelines:**
- List maximum 5-10 specific items
- Use bullet points or numbered list
- Be objective (not emotional or accusatory)
- Don't blame or criticize anyone
- Reference court documents and dates

### Step 5: Review & Validate

The system will automatically:
- Check for completeness
- Flag vague or frivolous requests
- Suggest corrections
- Confirm legal compliance

**Validation Checks:**
- ✓ Information-seeking (not opinion)
- ✓ Non-frivolous scope
- ✓ No accusations or blame
- ✓ No third-party PII
- ✓ Correct authority identified

### Step 6: Choose Export Format

Select how to submit your RTI:

#### Digital Formats:

- **PDF (Recommended)**
  - Professional appearance
  - Accepted by most courts
  - Easy to email
  - File size: Small

- **Word Document (.docx)**
  - Editable after export
  - Make last-minute changes
  - Some PIOs prefer

- **Email Format**
  - Paste directly in email
  - No attachment needed
  - For online submissions

#### Physical Formats:

- **Postal Format (Print-Ready)**
  - Optimized for printing
  - Includes address field layout
  - Space for signature
  - Ready to mail with acknowledgment slips

### Step 7: Submit Your Request

#### Online Submission:
1. Email PDF/Word to PIO
2. Include subject: "RTI Request - [Case Number]"
3. Request email receipt/tracking
4. Keep screenshot of sent email

#### Postal Submission:
1. Print both copies (one for PIO, one for you)
2. Address envelope to:
   ```
   [PIO Name]
   [Court Name]
   [Complete Address]
   ```
3. Send by registered post or speed post
4. Keep receipt number for tracking

#### Online Portal Submission:
1. Visit court website
2. Use official RTI submission portal
3. Upload PDF and applicant details
4. Get reference number

## RTI Templates

### Template 1: Missing Court Records

**Use Case:** Court documents are lost, damaged, or unavailable

**Sample Template:**
```
Subject: RTI Request - Request for Missing Records (Case No. 123/2023)

Dear [PIO Name],

I am writing to request information under the Right to Information Act, 2005, 
regarding Case Number 123/2023 pending before [Court Name].

INFORMATION REQUESTED:
1. Copy of the judgment/order dated [date]
2. List of all documents filed in this case
3. Certified copy of appeal memorandum, if available
4. Reasons for non-availability of any document

This case involves [brief nature], and the missing records are essential 
for further proceedings.

I shall be grateful for early action on this request.

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
[Contact Details]
```

### Template 2: Case Transfer History

**Use Case:** Understanding case movement between courts

**Sample Template:**
```
Subject: RTI Request - Case Transfer History (Case No. 123/2023)

Dear [PIO Name],

I am writing to request information under the Right to Information Act, 2005, 
regarding the transfer history of Case Number 123/2023.

INFORMATION REQUESTED:
1. Complete transfer history of the case with dates
2. Reasons for transfer as per transfer orders
3. Court receiving the case after transfer
4. Copy of transfer orders and reference numbers
5. Status of case with current court

I require this information to track the case proceedings across courts.

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
[Contact Details]
```

### Template 3: Hearing Transcripts

**Use Case:** Obtaining records of court proceedings

**Sample Template:**
```
Subject: RTI Request - Hearing Transcripts (Case No. 123/2023)

Dear [PIO Name],

I am writing to request information under the Right to Information Act, 2005,
regarding hearing records in Case Number 123/2023.

INFORMATION REQUESTED:
1. Transcript of hearing held on [date]
2. List of witnesses examined in this case
3. Dates of all hearings held so far
4. Adjournment orders with reasons
5. Pending hearing schedule if any

Kindly provide available court records and transcripts.

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
[Contact Details]
```

## Submitting Your Request

### Preferred Methods (in order):

#### 1. Email to PIO (Fastest)
- Fastest response (digital trail)
- Easy to track
- Always request read receipt
- Keep copy of email sent

#### 2. Speed Post / Registered Post
- Creates legal proof
- Slower but documented
- Verify address beforehand
- Retain receipt number

#### 3. Online Portal (If Available)
- Some courts have RTI portals
- Automatic tracking
- Instant confirmation
- Check court website

```
To: [PIO Email Address]

regarding Case Number [Case Number] pending before [Court Name].

[Your Name]
[Phone Number] 
[Email Address]
```

## Appeal Process

### When to Appeal:

You can appeal if:
- PIO denies your request
- Response is incomplete
- Information received is not what was requested
- No response within 30 days

### First Appeal (Under Section 19):

**Who:** Chief Public Information Officer (CPIO) of the court

**Timeline:** Within 90 days of denial or non-response

**Process:**
1. Write appeal highlighting deficiencies
2. Reference RTI rejection letter
3. Submit to CPIO
4. CPIO must decide within 30 days

**Appeal Template:**
```
Subject: First Appeal under RTI Act - [Case Number]

Dear [CPIO Name],

I am submitting this First Appeal against the RTI denial letter 
dated [date] issued regarding Case Number [Case Number].

GROUNDS FOR APPEAL:
1. [Reason for disputing denial]
2. [Relevant RTI Act section that supports your request]
3. [Information showing request is legitimate]

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS:
- Original RTI request
- PIO denial letter
- Relevant court orders

I request reconsideration of my request and justifying denial 
under specific provisions of RTI Act.

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
```

### Second Appeal (Under Section 19(6)):

**Who:** Central Information Commission (CIC)

**Timeline:** Within 90 days of First Appeal rejection

**Process:**
1. Prepare comprehensive appeal
2. Submit to CIC website/office
3. Your case may proceed to hearing
4. CIC issues binding decision

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is there a fee for RTI requests?**
A: No. RTI requests are free. PIO may charge ₹2/page for copying. Postal charges are your responsibility.

**Q: Can I submit an RTI anonymously?**
A: Yes, but PIOs may refuse and ask for identification. Providing details ensures smoother processing.

**Q: How many RTI requests can I file?**
A: Unlimited. However, frivolous repeated requests may be rejected.

**Q: What if PIO refuses my request?**
A: PIO must provide written reason citing specific RTI Act sections. You can file First Appeal within 90 days.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What file formats are accepted?**
A: Most courts accept PDF, Word, email, or postal. Check your court's website for preferences.

**Q: Can I edit my submission after sending?**
A: No. Submit another request if changes are needed. Reference the original request in your new one.

**Q: How do I know if PIO received my email?**
A: Request "read receipt" or email tracking. Call court office to confirm receipt.

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: "Invalid Court Name"

**Solution:**
- Use exact court name from official website
- Include city/state
- Reference: "District Court, Mumbai" not "Mumbai Court"
- Check spelling carefully

### Issue: "Information Not Specific"

**Solution:**
- Avoid general queries
- Be itemized: "1) ..., 2) ..., 3) ..."
- Reference case number and dates
- Avoid "I want all information about the case"

### Issue: "Frivolous Request"

**Solution:**
- Avoid accusations or blame
- Don't demand opinions
- Focus on factual information
- Don't request third-party private information

### Issue: "Unable to Find PIO"

**Solution:**
1. Visit court website
2. Look for "RTI Queries" page
3. Call court office: "+91-[number]"
4. Email to general court email with RTI in subject

### Issue: "Still No Response After 30 Days"

**Solution:**
1. Check postal delivery (postal requests)
2. Call PIO office to confirm receipt
3. Send reminder email with original request
4. File First Appeal after 40 days

### Issue: "PIO Asking for More Information"

**Solution:**
- Clarify but don't weaken request
- Provide case number and dates
- Reference any public documents
- Respond quickly (within 5 days)

## Important Legal Notes

### Your Rights:
- Right to access information under RTI Act, 2005
- Right to appeal if denied
- Right to receive information in desired format
- Protection after filing (no retaliation)

### Your Responsibilities:
- File legitimate requests (not frivolous)
- Don't request private information
- Don't blame or accuse officials
- Respect confidentiality limitations

### Limitations:
- Information may be partial due to privacy laws
- Some sensitive information is protected
- Court may refuse certain requests per RTI Act Section 8
- Charges may apply for voluminous information
